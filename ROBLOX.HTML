Roblox is an online platform where players can both play and create games, offering millions of user-made experiences like obstacle courses, roleplay worlds, and action games across devices such as PC, mobile, and consoles. It includes a game creation tool called Roblox Studio, which lets users design their own games using the Lua programming language, and it features a virtual currency called Robux for buying avatar items and in-game perks. Roblox is also highly social, allowing players to chat, add friends, and join multiplayer games, but it’s important to stay safe by not sharing personal information and using built-in safety features
